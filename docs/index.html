ON-DEVICE AI

Wallpaper DNA & Visual Intelligence

Extracting rich metadata in under 10ms with zero network footprint.

🎨

Color & Luminance Extraction

Calculates exact dominant & secondary RGB hex values, luminance brightness index (0.0 to 1.0), and true dark mode flags.

⚡

OLED Purity & Darkener

Detects pixel black ratios and provides pure black floor crushing (#000000) to maximize battery longevity on AMOLED screens.

🏷️

Style & Mood Tagging

Classifies edge density, saturation, and contrast into recognized genres (Cyberpunk, Nature, Minimalist, Sci-Fi, Anime, Abstract) and moods.

🔍

64-bit DCT Perceptual Hash

Identifies exact and near-duplicates regardless of compression, resizing, or cropping using Hamming distance matrix calculation.
